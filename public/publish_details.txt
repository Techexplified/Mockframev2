MockFrame - Instant Device Mockups

Tagline:
Create stunning device mockups directly from your Figma frames in seconds.

Description:
MockFrame is an ultra-fast, premium Figma plugin that instantly wraps your designs into beautiful, high-quality device mockups. Stop exporting frames and dealing with complex mockup tools. With MockFrame, you can generate stunning presentations directly inside your Figma canvas.

✨ Features:
- Latest Devices: Supports the newest flagship models including iPhone 16 Pro, Galaxy S24 Ultra, Pixel 9 Pro, and many more.
- Premium Aesthetics: Choose between Light, Dark, or Transparent modes with customizable options like shadows, reflections, and rounded corners.
- Local Image Uploads: Don't have your design in a frame? Easily drag & drop or upload local images directly into the plugin.
- Seamless Integration: Just click a frame on your canvas, pick a device, and click "Insert Mockup". Your mockup is instantly added to your Figma file as a high-quality, scalable image.
- Ultra-Clean UI: Designed with a modern, glassmorphism interface that feels like a native part of your premium design workflow.
